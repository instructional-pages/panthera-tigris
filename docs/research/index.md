# Research dossier

The cited research behind the course. Every claim on the site must trace back to a claim here,
and every claim here cites [sources](sources.md), which also says which sources are trusted and
how disagreements are handled. The user reviews the dossier before `docs/LESSONS.md` is written.

Each topic file teaches its terms first, then the facts, then lists its **Disagreements** and
what is still **Open** (to check before it can go on the site).
